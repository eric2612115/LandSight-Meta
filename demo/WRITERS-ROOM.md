# Writers' room on the film (2026-11-02)

## Pitch: Twelve on the bill

**Logline.** A landscape firm drops a developer's whole messy folder into Landsight, and every number in it, from the twelve camphors on the bill to the building-code shortfall, is pulled on a string back to a mark on the drawing.

**Cold open.** 0.0–1.5 Black. Room tone only. 1.5–3.5 A spreadsheet row fills the frame, nothing else: `Camphor tree, cal. 10 cm | 12 | NT$ 5,000`. The "12" is JetBrains Mono at 160 px; the cursor blinks on it. One keyboard click as the cell is selected, no music. 3.5–6.5 Hard cut to overhead paper: the printed L-1 Planting Plan (title block reads GREENFIELD RESIDENCES · SYNTHETIC SAMPLE), near-white paper, a yellow highlighter ticks the eight frontage canopies one by one, a squeak per tree, five-bar gate tally in the margin: four bars, a slash, three bars. Eight. The hand stops. 6.5–8.0 Cut to black, card in mono: `12 ≠ 8`, a single flat thud under it. Voice, dry, after the thud: "Twelve on the bill. Eight on the drawing." No cause is given. The viewer does not learn why until 2:46.

**The one image.** The eighth tree at 1:05: near-white paper in dark chrome, twelve red arcs of a canopy with seven red gap dots between them, and a single cobalt line snapping the circle shut while, in the panel beside it, Muse Spark's JSON reads `is_one_object: true · confidence 0.95` and the tally in the corner still says `drawn 7` because nobody has clicked yet.

**Meta tooling moment.** Three places, all read by colour (cobalt = Spark, violet = SAM, graphite = geometry). (1) The drop at 0:22: the chips that needed a look are cobalt, and the viewer sees Spark deciding sheet kinds (ELEVATION, ENLARGED PLAN, scale mismatch) and reading a raster scan's title block, so the model is doing triage, not captioning. (2) The eye at 0:58: the exact crop Landsight sent, the structured JSON answer typing itself with a `why`, grounding points landing on the crop, and the tag `Muse Spark called on 1 of 8 · 7.7 s`, which shows the routing (geometry first, Spark only on what geometry could not close) and that the product still hands the count to a person. (3) Lines to colour at 1:32: `0 masks` on line work stated as a fact, then Spark reading the legend to name the colours SAM is prompted with, SAM's masks within one percent of CAD by type, and the legend swatches SAM also returned struck out on screen. Together they show two Meta models orchestrated around their measured limits rather than called once.

### Risks
- The repo today has the pipeline, rules, Spark and SAM wrappers and a director-mode server, but no frontend/, no landsight/ingest, no landsight/checks and no package generator; beats 4 (drop), 10 (bill) and 11 (thirty strings) are shots of code that does not exist yet, so the build order in docs/PACKAGE.md is the film's critical path and beat 11 must be the first thing cut to the eval's real numbers if the scoreboard is not 30/30 (print the real line; never round it).
- The gauge figures (268 of 297, 306, 436) are script v3's and predate the v2 package (pit downgrade E12, fire-lane and road-reserve trees E08/E09); the film must carry whatever GET /api/rules prints after the answer key locks, and the voice line must be re-recorded if the shape changes (e.g. if two trees no longer fix it).
- PACKAGE.md (E17: hedge billed in metres, 27) and SCRIPT v3 (hedge × 150, short by 32) disagree on the hedge row of the bill; this pitch follows PACKAGE.md because it is the generator contract; whoever writes the bill generator must pick one and the voice line follows it.
- SAM 3.1 has been probed only on a clean flat-colour render; the package's Presentation Plan (Colour).pdf must be generated the same way (no texture, no hatch over colour) or beat 8 goes back to canopies only; the legend swatches must be excluded on screen, as the probe showed they come back for every colour prompt.
- Director mode must be real: every scene in the film is reachable at /?director=1 with its own URL, nothing is composited in post beyond captions and sound; if a judge in the top five opens the live product and the scene plays differently, the Demo and Depth scores both fall.
- Narration is about 245 words over 180 s with roughly 55 s of silence; an ElevenLabs v4 read must be directed dry, low and slow (about 110 wpm, no advertising lift) and the voice credited on the end card; use it now as the scratch track to lock the cut, and replace with Eric's own single-take read only if it is as flat and as well-timed.
- English only on screen: the species data file's Chinese column must never render in any panel, chip, tooltip or bill row, including the aligned name in beat 10; one stray glyph on a live judge's screen breaks the rule.
- The cold-open paper needs a real printout of the synthetic L-1 with SYNTHETIC SAMPLE in the title block and Eric's own hand; if that cannot be shot cleanly, use the on-screen spreadsheet and the app's own eight-canopy highlight instead of paper, and keep the eight squeaks.
- Nothing in the film may say or imply first or only, name a rival, or show a fake miscount; the recorded naive Muse Spark runs all answered 8 and the film never claims otherwise; the only wrong number is the developer's bill.
- Length: the timeline is exactly 3:00 with no slack; every added second must come out of beats 4 or 11, never out of the silence before the Confirm click at 1:14.

## Pitch: Landsight — Four Trees Nobody Drew
