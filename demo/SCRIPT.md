## 1. The film in one line, and the thesis

A builder drops a property developer's thirty-file landscape package into
Landsight and, in three minutes, the bill's twelve camphors, a hedge priced as one row, a tree pit on a slab that
shrinks every large tree, and a hidden spreadsheet are each walked back to a mark on a drawing or a sentence in a
document — and the product grades itself on the owner's own statement, with a package the checks were never tuned on
beside it.

The problems the film shows and how each is solved on screen (from THESIS.md):

| The problem the owner lives with | What Landsight does on screen | What the viewer concludes |
|---|---|---|
| The package is unreviewable as delivered: thirty files, a backup, an Excel 97 quote, a zip, a scan, a deck, four bill revisions. | Every file gets a verdict and a reason; the governing bill and the governing revision are named; junk says why it is junk (0:03–0:05, the cascade insert). | "It can take the real folder." |
| The drawings don't say what they seem to: a tree is twelve loose lines, a hedge is a note, a label carries a species. | Geometry closes and measures what it can; Muse Spark reads the rest and says why; nothing a model says is counted until a person confirms it (0:11–0:47). | "It reads the drawing the way an estimator does, and shows its work." |
| Areas on a coloured picture, not a line drawing — and a bill that charges for more paving than the drawing shows. | The bill's paving against the drawing's, and the money, before the trick; SAM 3.1 finds nothing in the line work and the paving on the colour plan (prompted with the legend's words as Muse Spark read them, C-38 — a fact the film leaves to the Run); measured against the drawing (0:47–1:02). | "It measures the pretty picture too, and the money comes with it." |
| The rules nobody checks until the permit office does: greening short, a pit detail that downgrades every large tree, a fire lane. | Required vs provided, the pit rule from L-6, the designer's lever (a species change), the conditional case handed to the architect (1:02–1:25). | "It catches the thing that would have cost the permit." |
| The bill drifts from the drawing: twelve billed, eight drawn; a rate far from the firm's own; a subtotal that skips a row; a hidden sheet. | Rows aligned to drawn items, the money in dollars, the trace to the bill's cell and the drawing's own note (1:25–1:50). | "It finds the money — in both directions." |
| The PDFs you are expected to read yourself. | Ask the package: a question, a cited answer that says the sources do not settle it, the sheet opened on the sentence, one click that files it (1:50–2:03). | "It read the package so I don't have to — and I can check it." |
