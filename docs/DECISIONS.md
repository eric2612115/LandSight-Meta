## D-01 Licence: AGPL-3.0 — Proposed (2026-11-01)

Question: which licence for the public repository the hackathon requires.
Decision: AGPL-3.0.
Trade-off: AGPL keeps a hosted copy from being closed off, which protects the product idea; it deters some

## D-02 Demo data is synthetic — Proposed (2026-11-01)

Question: what drawings the demo, the tests and the public repo use.
Decision: every drawing in the repo is generated by `landsight/synth` (DXF via ezdxf) with a known answer key.
No real project's drawings or documents are used anywhere in this repository or sent to any API (the hackathon
waiver grants Meta a perpetual licence to anything used in the event).
Trade-off: synthetic drawings are tidier than real ones, so the generator adds the usual messiness on purpose; in
exchange the answer key is exact and the repo is clean to publish.

## D-03 Division of labour between geometry, Muse Spark and SAM 3.1 — Proposed (2026-11-01)

Question: which tool does which job, given the spike results of 2026-11-01 (`spike/`).
Findings: on a rendered CAD line drawing SAM 3.1 returned no boxes for "tree", "tree symbol", "circle",
"rectangle" or "text label" at any line weight, nor on a simulated scan; on a filled, coloured rendering it found
the three canopies for "circle" (confidence 0.965) but not for "tree". Muse Spark 1.3 (vision, JSON schema) counted
the three symbols, told solid from dashed, read 「樟樹 typ.」「樟樹 ×8」「小葉欖仁 @3m」 and quoted 10 trees, in
7.7 s for 4.3k tokens.
Decision:
- Geometry (ezdxf + shapely) measures: lengths, areas, counts of closed shapes; it snaps gaps up to a tolerance.
- Muse Spark is the eye and the reader: it decides which loose segments form one object when geometry cannot,
  reads labels, "typ.", "×N", "@spacing", leader lines and plant schedules, and explains each count.
- SAM 3.1 works on filled regions: coloured presentation plans, hatch fills, scans — planting beds and paving
  areas for area take-off. It is not asked to read line work.
Trade-off: Spark calls cost seconds and tokens per candidate, so geometry runs first and Spark only sees what
geometry could not close; SAM gets only the drawings it can see.

## D-04 AI proposes, a person confirms — Proposed (2026-11-01)

Question: can the quote be produced without a human step.
Decision: no. Every counted object carries an ID drawn on the plan; the estimator confirms (or edits) the list,
and only then is the quotation generated. Where geometry, Spark and the plant schedule disagree, the item is
flagged, never silently resolved.
Trade-off: one more screen; in exchange every number in the quote traces back to a mark on the drawing.

## D-05 "Regulation check", not "legal advice" — Proposed (2026-11-01)

Question: how to name the rules feature.
Decision: 法規檢核 / regulation check: the tool compares drawing quantities against named articles (cited with
their numbers) and reports pass / fail / needs review; a licensed architect or landscape professional signs off.
Trade-off: none for the product; it avoids the appearance of unlicensed legal advice.

## D-06 The user is the design firm's owner reviewing a developer's package — Proposed (2026-11-01)

Question: whose job does Landsight do: a contractor's estimator writing a quote (script v1), or a landscape
design firm's owner reviewing a developer's package (CAD + Excel bill) before taking the job.
Decision: the owner. Inputs are the drawings and the developer's bill; outputs are flags (quantity: bill vs
drawing; price: bill vs the firm's own price list; code: pass / fail / needs review), each pointing at marks on
the drawing. Nothing is quoted; the firm decides.
Trade-off: a bill reader and a name aligner are extra work; in exchange the story has one user, the species
swap in the code beat is that user's own lever, and the three judges' persona objection is answered.

## D-08 Synthetic site v2: one site, one answer key — Proposed (2026-11-02)

## D-09 Gap closing by direction-aware endpoint bridging — Proposed (2026-11-02)

Question: how to close loose canopies without joining neighbours.
Decision: free ends (degree-one nodes) are bridged to the nearest free end within the snap tolerance (default
0.25 m) when both ends point at each other within 50 degrees, else to a line they point at within 25 degrees
(T-gap); gaps under 6 cm close regardless of direction. Bridges are added, original geometry is never moved. The
spike's buffer trick is not used. Open line work is clustered by buffer-union with eps 0.6 m.
Trade-off: a gap larger than the tolerance always goes to Spark; the direction rule is what keeps the 40 cm tree
from being bridged to its neighbour 13.6 cm away.

## D-10 What geometry calls a canopy, an area and sheet furniture — Proposed (2026-11-02)

Decision: a canopy is an unhatched closed shape with circularity >= 0.85 and 0.5-150 m². An area item is a hatched
closed shape with a label (leader or text inside) or a legend swatch of the same pattern; paving / lawn / shrub bed
by keyword (磚 石 鋪 混凝土 P.C. → paving; 草皮 草坪 → lawn; else shrub bed). The legend, title block and schedule are
the smallest connected group of line work (holes filled) containing 圖例/LEGEND, 圖名/工程名稱/圖號/比例, or
植栽表/數量表/SCHEDULE, capped at 30% of the sheet. Leaders are open lines with a free end at a text.

## D-11 Labels: typ. propagates, group counts are a fourth source — Proposed (2026-11-02)

Decision: a species label on one canopy applies to every canopy of the same area (±15%); 「typ.」 never multiplies.
「8-樟樹」 becomes `label_count` and must agree with geometry, Spark and the schedule. Unlabelled canopies take the
species of the legend's single tree symbol.

## D-12 Rows along a line are counted by geometry only — Proposed (2026-11-02)

Decision: an open cluster with an "@spacing" label (leader or within 3 m) is counted as floor(L/s)+1 per drawn line
(雙排 with one drawn line counts it twice) and is never sent to Spark. Spacing under 1 m is a hedge, otherwise a tree
row.

## D-13 Agreement and the spark_count definition — Proposed (2026-11-02)

Decision: spark_count = closed canopies + what Spark says the open candidates of that item stand for; None when
Spark reviewed none. An item is agreed only if every available source (geometry, Spark, schedule, group label)
gives the same number (areas and lengths within 1%) and no member is an open outline; only agreed items get a
quote_count. Items with one source (paving, not on the schedule) count as agreed. In a schedule dispute
proposed_count follows the drawing (real plans state that the plan governs).

## D-14 How Spark is asked — Proposed (2026-11-02)

Decision: one open candidate per call, rendered red with its ID tag in a crop with at least 6 m of context, plus the
measured facts in the prompt (piece count, gaps, linetype, size, neighbouring canopy area); labels are read from
the image, not passed as text. At most 10 calls per sheet; sheets Spark classifies as non-plan get no further calls
and count nothing. If classification fails the sheet is treated as a plan and the failure is noted. Fixtures are
keyed on the image sha256 with a DXF content hash as fallback.

Trade-off: the film script calls it T-07; either the script changes or the gap list is reversed.

## D-18 The greening check uses the hedge area geometry measures — Proposed (2026-11-02)

Question: PLAN.md's §302 numbers assume a 16 m² hedge (provided 268, +2 trees 306.5, swap 435.6).
Decision: the hedge counts as the band between its two drawn lines, 27.0 × 0.4 = 10.8 m² (Gi 0.5 → 5.4), as
`geometry.hedge_bands` measures it. On the m4 site: required 297.0, provided 265.9 (FAIL), +2 Camphor 304.3, swap
432.1. The film (beat 8) uses these numbers, or the synth widens the hedge to 0.6 m if the round numbers matter.
Trade-off: the film's 268 / 306 / 436 change to 266 / 304 / 432; in exchange every number on screen is measured.

## D-19 Fix b is the smallest swap: one tree — Proposed (2026-11-02)

Question: the script swaps 2 of 8 camphors for a second native species.
Decision: the rules engine reports the cheapest swap. Because ra counts native trees of all trees once there are
two species, and camphor is native, swapping one tree already gives ra = 1, α = 1.3, 432.1 (a non-native row such
as Madagascar almond needs two). The film says "swap one", or keeps "swap two" as the designer's choice (same
432.1; the API returns the objects to re-tone).
Trade-off: none in the code; the script line changes.

## D-20 SAM 3.1 is asked by colour, and the type comes from the drawing — Proposed (2026-11-02)

Question: can SAM give bed and paving areas by type (docs/research/sam-probe.md)?
Decision: yes for areas (beds and paving within 1%), no for types: material words return nothing. SAM gets colour
and shape phrases ("green rectangle", "grey area", "beige area"); each mask takes the type of the outline it lies
in (or, on a PDF, of the legend swatch of its colour); legend swatches are dropped. Beat 7 ships. Hedges stay with
geometry (+33% on a 10 px band).
Trade-off: one SAM call per legend colour instead of one per material; the type is never SAM's guess.

## D-29 The enlarged plan is drawn 2x in model space — Proposed (2026-11-02)

Question: how "title block 1:200, scale bar 1:100" [E16] can be a geometric fact in a DXF, where model space is metres.
Decision: L-5's content is drawn at 2 model units per metre with sheet furniture the size of the 1:200 sheets, as

## D-34 Grounding points are an optional schema field, shown with their measured error — Proposed (2026-11-02)

Question: the eye beat wants Muse Spark's points on the T-08 crop (D-24 cut them unless re-recorded).
Decision: GROUP_SCHEMA gets an optional `points` array ({x, y}, 0–1000 on the crop, x from the left, y from the top;
clamped, at most 12) and the prompt asks for up to 12 on the red segments. The T-08 call was re-recorded once
(group_and_read__f7c68d…, 4.9 s, 1,024 output tokens): 12 points on a ring. `Item.spark.points` gives each point in
crop pixels and drawing metres with its distance to the nearest red segment; 6 of 12 lie within 0.3 m, the farthest
0.75 m (Spark's ring is about 2.3 m tall against 1.9 m wide for a 2 m canopy). With no points the field is null and
`points_status`/`points_note` say why. FixtureSpark now keeps the newest recording when two share an image or key.
Trade-off: the points are close, not exact; the film can show them as Spark's, with the measured distance.

circle" scored 0.89–0.92; same masks, lower confidence.

## D-37 The §5 table 1 pit downgrade, and the result shows both states — Proposed (2026-11-02)

Question: the gauge twist (D-24) needs the pit rule to run from a recorded read of L-6.
Decision: on artificial ground (title block ARTIFICIAL GROUND / ON SLAB, the detail's `on_slab`, or a parameter), a
large tree in a pit of [1.5, 4.0) m² counts as a small tree (Gi 1.5 → 1.0); under 1.5 m², or soil below the class
minimum (1.0 m large, 0.7 m small, 0.4 m shrub, 0.1 m grass: the roof / terrace column, used for a slab), the tree is
